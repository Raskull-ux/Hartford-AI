How a session works

No account to make and no app to download.

  1. Book below

    Pick FIX or POUR, tell me what is going on, and choose a day and time that works for you.

  2. I confirm your time

    I reply to lock in the time. For video sessions I send a call link. For in-person POUR, we agree on a public spot in the Hartford area.

  3. Pay $50 to hold the slot

    Use PayPal, Cash App, Venmo or Zelle. Payment holds your time.

  4. We work on it together

    You share your screen or your pour, I walk you through it step by step, and you leave knowing how to do it again. You get a short written recap after.
